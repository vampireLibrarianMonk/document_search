## Decision Workflow Diagram

- Get multiple quotes from contractors (Virginia Roofing, All Day Roofing, BRAX)
- Review pricing, materials (TPO, EPDM options), and warranty terms
- Consider additional requirements (parking/staging area, wood blocking repairs)
- Evaluate neighborhood coordination needs (parapet wall installation requires neighbor consent)
- Select contractor based on cost, warranty, and material preferences
- Schedule work after mutual agreement and deposit if required
- Prepare site: Provide parking/staging area, protect interior property
- Complete installation with agreed-upon materials and warranty coverage
- Final inspection and cleanup (broom clean condition)
