## Proposed Materials & Warranties

*   **BRAX Roofing** recommends a **TPO membrane** system with new 1" poly ISO insulation for its waterproofing, heat-welded seams, and reflective surface.
*   **All Day Roofing** options include **60mil TPO** (15-20 yr warranty), **75mil EPDM** (20-30 yr warranty), and **90mil EPDM** (30-50 yr warranty).
*   **Virginia Roofing** specifies its repair price is based on using **EPDM rubber & associated materials** with a 1-year workmanship guarantee.
*   Warranties range from **1-year workmanship** (Virginia Roofing repairs) to up to a **50-year manufacturer warranty** (All Day's premium option).

## Key Considerations & Exclusions

*   **Staging Area**: Both Virginia Roofing and notes from other proposals state a parking/staging area MUST be provided next to the building.
*   **Excluded Costs**: Prices typically exclude repairs to wood blocking, disconnecting/reconnecting rooftop equipment, lightning rods, and tapered insulation (new roof follows existing slope).
*   **Material Availability**: Contracts may allow for substitute materials if specified items are unavailable, with cost increases paid by the owner.
*   **Homeowner Responsibilities**: Contractors are not responsible for protecting interior walls/personal property or hazardous substances like mold/asbestos.

## Decision Process Workflow

*   **Step 1**: Review all received quotes and proposals for scope, price, materials, and warranties.
*   **Step 2**: Verify contractor licensure (e.g., MHIC: 109580 for Firestone/BRAX) and check references.
*   **Step 3**: Decide between full replacement or repairs, and choose material type (TPO vs. EPDM) based on budget and warranty needs.
*   **Step 4**: Coordinate with neighboring homeowners if parapet wall installation is chosen, as their consent is needed for tie-ins.
*   **Step 5**: Secure a signed, written agreement detailing cost, materials, work, and completion date before any work begins.

## Timeline & Next Steps

*   **Immediate**: Proposals from Virginia Roofing are valid for 30 days. BRAX's quote includes a 15% discount.
*   **Pre-Work**: Schedule a final walkthrough, confirm cash amount, and ensure a staging area is arranged.
*   **During Work**: Be prepared for potential material price changes upon delivery, with invoices justifying any differences from quotes.
*   **Post-Completion**: Collect all warranty documents, ensure work areas are left "broom clean," and update your HOA and home insurance.
