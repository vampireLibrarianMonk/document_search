## Workflow Diagram

1. Obtain quotes from multiple contractors
2. Review proposals
   - Compare materials (TPO, EPDM, etc.)
   - Compare warranty terms
   - Compare pricing and payment options
3. Consult with HOA (if applicable)
   - Get approval for proposed work
   - Coordinate with neighboring properties (for parapet walls)
4. Select preferred contractor and proposal
5. Sign contract and provide deposit
6. Schedule installation date
7. Prepare site (provide staging area)
8. Contractor performs roof replacement
9. Final inspection and approval
10. Make final payment

## Summary

- Multiple reputable contractors provided quotes
- TPO and EPDM are recommended flat roof systems
