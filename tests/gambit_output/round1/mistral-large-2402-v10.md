## Decision Process Workflow
1. **Get Quotes**: Reach out to multiple contractors for quotes.
2. **Review Quotes**: Compare prices, materials, and warranty terms.
3. **Check References**: Ask for and check references from each contractor.
4. **Select Contractor**: Choose a contractor based on quotes, references, and personal comfort.
5. **Schedule Work**: Coordinate with the contractor to schedule the roof replacement.

## Timeline
- **Quote Collection**: 1-2 weeks
- **Review and Reference Check**: 1 week
- **Contractor Selection**: 1 day
- **Scheduling and Preparation**: 1-2 weeks
- **Roof Replacement**: Varies by contractor, typically 1-3 days
