# Roof Replacement Options & Contractor Evaluation

## Roof Replacement Overview
- Flat roof replacement is needed for Patrick Flanagan’s residence
- Multiple contractors submitted proposals including Virginia Roofing, BRAX Roofing, and All Day Roofing & More
- The roof system is currently tied to adjacent units with similar wear and tear
- Proposals include TPO and EPDM membrane systems with warranties up to 50 years

## Contractor Quotes & Materials
- **Virginia Roofing**: Quoted $2,289 for emergency patch repairs
- **BRAX Roofing**: Quotes range from $10,595 for TPO membrane to $24,350 for a 3-home replacement
- **All Day Roofing & More**: Quotes from $12,800 to $20,996 using TPO or EPDM systems
- BRAX Roofing recommends Firestone TPO with a 10-year manufacturer and 10-year workmanship warranty

## Warranty Comparisons
- **TPO Systems**: 10-50 year manufacturer warranties (BRAX: 10 years, All Day Roofing: up to 30-50 years)
- **EPDM Systems**: 25-50 year warranties (All Day Roofing: 25-30 years for 75 mil and 90 mil EPDM)
- Workmanship warranties range from 1 year (Virginia Roofing) to 10 years (BRAX Roofing)

## Additional Terms & Conditions
- Virginia Roofing requires a staging area and excludes work on wood blocking
- Firestone and BRAX roofing agreements include homeowner responsibility for hazardous materials
- BRAX Roofing requires coordination with neighboring homeowners for parapet wall integration

## Project Timeline Overview
- Average project duration depends on roofing type and site conditions (~4-6 weeks)
- Weather delays could affect timeline with contract adjustments for unavailability
- A roof that separates from adjacent units may require special coordination

## Decision Workflow
- Initial inspection and quote review
- Compare materials, warranties, and additional terms
- Consult with neighbors (if parapet walls involved)
- Select contractor and confirm proposal terms in writing
- Schedule work completion
