## Materials Proposed
- **TPO (Thermoplastic Olefin)**
  - Excellent waterproofing, heat-welded seams, energy efficiency, durability, low maintenance
- **Firestone**
  - National Roofing Contractors Association member, MHIC: 109580
- **EPDM (Ethylene Propylene Diene Monomer)**
  - Durable, flexible, resistant to weather and UV rays
- **RubberGard**
  - High-performance EPDM roofing system
