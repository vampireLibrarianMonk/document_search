*   **Common Exclusions:** Repairs/replacement of wood blocking, disconnecting/reconnecting roof-top equipment/lightning rods/gas lines, tapered insulation (new roof follows existing slope).
*   **Homeowner Responsibilities:** Provide staging/parking area, protect personal property, obtain necessary permits/permissions (e.g., for parapet tie-ins).
*   **Decision Factors:** Material type (TPO vs. EPDM) and warranty length, total cost, need for neighbor coordination, inclusion of necessary equipment (forklift) and ancillary repairs (balcony).
