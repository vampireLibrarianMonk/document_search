## Decision Process Workflow
1. **Get Quotes**: Obtain quotes from multiple contractors.
2. **Review Quotes**: Compare prices, materials, and warranty terms.
3. **Check References**: Verify contractor licenses and check customer reviews.
4. **Select Contractor**: Choose a contractor based on quote, references, and personal preference.
5. **Schedule Work**: Coordinate with the contractor to schedule the roof replacement.

## Timeline
- **Quote Collection**: 1-2 weeks
- **Review and Decision Making**: 1 week
- **Reference Checking**: 1 week
- **Scheduling and Preparation**: 1-2 weeks
- **Roof Replacement**: Dependent on contractor availability and project scope
