# Roof Replacement Options and Contractors

## Slide 1: Overview of Roof Replacement Options

- Three main roof replacement options considered: TPO, EPDM, and Firestone.
- Virginia Roofing Corporation, All Day Roofing & More LLC, and BRAX Roofing submitted proposals.
- Options range from 15–20 years to 30–50 years in warranty coverage.
- Prices vary based on material type and system complexity.

## Slide 2: Virginia Roofing Corporation

- Quoted $2,289 for minor roof repairs including caulking and patching.
- Offers 1-year guarantee and a separate quote for full replacement.
- Uses EPDM rubber with estimated 15–20 year useful life.
- No staging area or additional costs for repairs included.

## Slide 3: All Day Roofing & More LLC

- Three options offered for full roof replacement:
  - Option 1: TPO (15–20 year warranty) priced at $12,800.
  - Option 2: EPDM (20–30 year warranty) priced at $16,114.
  - Option 3: RubberGard 90 mil EPDM (30–50 year warranty) priced at $20,996.
- Includes labor, materials, and insulation for all options.
- Staging area must be provided next to the building.

## Slide 4: BRAX Roofing Proposal

- Parapet wall installation option available for $3,625 additional cost.
- Full roof replacement with TPO membrane and 1" insulation: $10,595.
- Three-home roof option if coordinated with neighboring homes: $24,350.
- Warranty includes 10-year workmanship and material coverage.

## Slide 5: Firestone Roofing (BRAX Partner)

- 10-year workmanship warranty and 10-year material warranty on TPO systems.
- Firestone products are Kynar 500 smooth finish with manufacturer-specified colors.
- Firestone is affiliated with the National Roofing Contractors Association and has valid MHIC licensing (109580).

## Slide 6: Timeline and Workflow

- Phase 1: Get roofing quotes and material specifications
- Phase 2: Compare warranty terms and contractor reputation
- Phase 3: Finalize decision with detailed pricing and materials
- Phase 4: Schedule work with required staging and inspections
- Phase 5: Final walkthrough and system inspection

## Slide 7: Summary and Contact

- Final decision should factor cost, warranty duration, and material quality.
- All Day Roofing offers longest warranty (30–50 years).
- BRAX includes optional parapet wall for better waterproofing.
- Virginia Roofing provides lower entry cost with minor repair quote.
