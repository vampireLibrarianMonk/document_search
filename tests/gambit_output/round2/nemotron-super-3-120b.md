# Roof Replacement Options Presentation

## Contractor Quote Summary
- All Day Roofing & More LLC: $12,800 (TPO), $16,114.80 (EPDM 75mil), $20,996.04 (RubberGard 90 mil EPDM)
- Virginia Roofing Corporation: Material quotes change upon delivery due to market pricing
- BRAX Roofing: $10,595 (TPO without parapet), $3,625 extra for parapet wall
- Flanagan Residence Proposal: 8 SQ flat roof with tapered insulation systems

## Material Options Available
- TPO: 15-20 year warranty (60mil) or 10-year workmanship + 10-year manufacturer
- EPDM: 75mil with 20-30 year warranty, 90 mil RubberGard with 30-50 year manufacturer warranty
- Firestone Building Products: TPO roofing system with National Roofing Contractors Association affiliation
- Insulation: 1" poly ISO insulation underneath TPO, slope board insulation (tapered system)

## Warranty Terms & Conditions
- BRAX Roofing: 10-year workmanship warranty through BRAX + 10-year manufacturer material warranty on TPO
- All Day Roofing: Options include 15-20 year, 20-30 year, and 30-50 year manufacturer warranties
- Virginia Roofing Corporation: Price based on Manufacturer's standard colors with Kynar 500 smooth finish only
- Note: Warranty details vary by material selection and contractor

## Project Timeline & Scheduling
- Virginia Roofing Corporation: Contract time extended for material unavailability delays
- Material pricing: Locked in only upon delivery due to market instability
- Virginia Roofing: Can provide invoice showing difference between quote and invoice for justification
- BRAX Roofing: Parapet wall installation requires consent from neighboring homeowners for tie-in areas

## Decision Workflow Process
- Obtain multiple contractor quotes for comparison
- Review materials, warranties, and pricing breakdowns
- Consider parapet wall requirements and neighbor coordination needs
- Select contractor based on warranty, price, and material preferences
- Schedule work contingent on material availability and pricing locks
- Confirm staging area and utility access requirements before commencement

## Additional Requirements & Notes
- Parking and staging area MUST be provided next to the building (Virginia Roofing Corporation)
- Price does not include repairs/replacement of wood blocking ($4.00 per lineal feet for 2x6 pressure treated)
- Price does not include disconnecting/reconnecting lightning rods, roof top equipment, electrical conduits, gas lines, lighting fixtures, or camera systems
- Note: A staging area for trucks and equipment must be provided next to the building (multiple contractors)
- Interior walls and personal property preparation is buyer's responsibility prior to contractor commencement
- Mold and related matters: Buyer agrees to indemnify contractor for hazardous substance claims
- No alteration to agreement valid without signed, written consent of both parties
- Agreement governed by laws of state where performed, except as preempted by federal law

## Final Recommendations
- Compare TPO vs EPDM options based on budget and desired warranty length
- Verify if parapet wall installation is needed and obtain neighbor consents early
- Confirm all exclusions (wood blocking, equipment reconnection, etc.) before signing
- Ensure staging area availability for contractor trucks and equipment
- Request detailed warranty sheets for HOA or insurance documentation purposes
- Consider long-term complications of connected roof systems when making decisions
- Thermal performance improvement with new 1" insulation layer beneath membrane
- Heat-welded seams on TPO provide superior leak resistance
- Reflective TPO surface offers better energy efficiency
- Low maintenance compared to older flat roof systems
