## Materials Proposed

- TPO (Thermoplastic Polyolefin)
  - Excellent waterproofing protection
  - Heat-welded seams for superior leak resistance
  - Better energy efficiency due to reflective surface
  - Long-term durability and flexibility
  - Low maintenance

- EPDM (Ethylene Propylene Diene Monomer)
  - Durable and long-lasting (up to 50-year warranty)
  - Resistant to extreme temperatures and weather conditions
  - Seamless installation with fewer seams
  - Cost-effective option

- Firestone Building Products
  - Firestone's TPO membrane flat roof system proposed by BRAX Roofing
