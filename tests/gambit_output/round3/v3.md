## Key Considerations & Warranty Summary
-   **Warranties Vary:** BRAX offers 10-year warranties, All Day offers 15-50 year material warranties depending on option, and VA Roofing's repair quote has a 1-year guarantee.
-   **Neighbor Coordination:** BRAX's parapet wall option requires consent from both neighboring homeowners.
-   **Material Stability:** Virginia Roofing notes that material quotes are not locked until delivery.
-   **Contract Terms:** Standard terms include contractor compliance with permits, removal of debris, and customer responsibility for utilities and access.

## Decision & Selection Workflow
1.  **Gather & Compare Quotes** (This Presentation).
2.  **Clarify Scope & Warranties:** Request detailed warranty sheets and confirm final material pricing.
3.  **Coordinate with Neighbors/HOA:** Discuss parapet wall option if separation is desired.
4.  **Select Contractor & Sign Agreement:** Sign written contract after finalizing price, scope, and timeline.
5.  **Schedule Work & Prepare Site:** Provide staging/parking area and ensure utility access.
