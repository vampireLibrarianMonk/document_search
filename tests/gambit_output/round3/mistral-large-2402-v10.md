## Decision Process
1. **Get Quotes**: Reach out to multiple contractors for quotes.
2. **Review Quotes**: Compare prices, materials, and warranty terms.
3. **Check References**: Ask for references and check online reviews.
4. **Select Contractor**: Choose a contractor based on quote, references, and comfort level.
5. **Schedule Work**: Coordinate with contractor to schedule the roof replacement.

## Timeline
- **Week 1-2**: Get quotes from contractors.
- **Week 3**: Review quotes, check references, and select contractor.
- **Week 4-6**: Schedule work and prepare for roof replacement.
