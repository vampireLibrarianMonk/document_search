# Roof Replacement Options

## Getting Quotes

- Contact multiple local roofing contractors
- Provide details about your roof (size, existing material, etc.)
- Request quotes for various roofing systems (TPO, EPDM, etc.)
- Inquire about warranties and workmanship guarantees
