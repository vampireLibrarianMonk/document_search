## Materials and Warranty Terms

- **TPO (Thermoplastic Polyolefin)**: 
  - Available with 15-50 year warranties depending on thickness (60mil to 90mil).
  - Heat-welded seams and energy-efficient reflective surface.
- **EPDM (Ethylene Propylene Diene Monomer)**: 
  - Available in 20-30 year and 25 year warranties.
  - Known for waterproofing and durability.
- **Firestone Product**:
  - Kynar 500 smooth finish with standard color warranty.
- **BRAX Roofing Warranty**:
  - 10-year workmanship warranty and 10-year material warranty on TPO.

## Included Work and Exclusions

- **Included**:
  - Installation of new insulation layers and waterproofing membranes.
  - Labor costs, glue, primer, and flashing as per specific quotes.
- **Exclusions**:
  - Repair or replacement of wood blocking not included.
  - Repairs not included unless quoted separately (e.g. wood blocking: $4.00/lineal foot).
  - No cost or service for lightning rod, gas line, or electrical conduit disconnects.

## Timeline and Contract Terms

- **Project Timeline**:

- **Contract Terms**:

## Decision Process Workflow

- **Step 1: Obtain Multiple Quotes**
  - Request proposals from several roofing contractors.
- **Step 2: Review Quotes**
  - Compare materials, warranties, prices, and included/excluded services.
- **Step 3: Select a Contractor**
  - Choose based on budget, materials, and warranty comfort.
- **Step 4: Schedule Work**
  - Coordinate staging areas and final delivery of materials with the selected contractor.
