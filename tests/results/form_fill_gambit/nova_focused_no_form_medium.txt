The proposed modification involves replacing the existing low-slope/flat roof with a new GAF LIBERTY™ SBS 2-ply Roofing System. This system is applied without the need for torches, open flames, hot asphalt, or messy solvent-based adhesives, making it safer and easier to install. The GAF LIBERTY™ SBS 2-ply Roofing System is a self-adhering roofing system that provides long-lasting protection for low-slope areas. It offers several benefits, including no fumes, proven technology, and a great warranty. The system is based on the same technology used in GAF's commercial modified bitumen roofing systems, which have been proven in the field for decades. The roofing system is also flexible, allowing for faster installation with minimal set-up or clean-up time. Additionally, it does not require special tools or equipment, reducing insurance premiums. The GAF LIBERTY™ SBS 2-ply Roofing System is available in several popular colors to complement the existing residential shingle roof.
