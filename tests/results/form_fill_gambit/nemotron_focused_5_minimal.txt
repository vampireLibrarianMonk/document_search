I am proposing to replace the existing flat roof at 12133 Tribune Street, Fairfax, VA 22033 using the GAF Liberty™ SBS Self-Adhering 2-Ply Roofing System as specified in the American Home Contractors proposal dated May 14, 2026. The project includes removal of the existing roofing material down to the sheathing, installation of new 1" ISO insulation board anchored with screws and plates (tapered as needed for drainage), and application of the Liberty™ SBS Self-Adhering Base/Ply Sheet and Cap Sheet. All flashings and edge metals will be installed to properly seal the roof perimeter and penetrations, and new pipe flashings will be added to all vent pipes. The system is self-adhering, requiring no torches, open flames, hot asphalt, or solvent-based adhesives during installation, which enhances safety and eliminates fumes and odors. The Liberty™ system is FM approved, UL classified, and meets Miami-Dade County, FBC, and Texas Department of Insurance standards (where applicable). It comes with a limited warranty against material defects for up to 15 years, with optional full-system guarantees available for up to 20 years against manufacturing defects and workmanship errors. The product is available in seven popular colors—Black, Hickory, Shakewood, Slate, Tan, Weathered Wood, and White—to complement the home’s existing aesthetic. All work will be performed by a licensed contractor (M.H.I.C. #31337-03, VA License #2705190396) in compliance with local building codes, and all debris will be cleaned and disposed of upon completion. The total cost for this scope of work is $7,200.00, and the contractor, American Home Contractors, LLC, maintains general liability insurance with limits of $1,000,000 per occurrence and $2,000,000 aggregate, as verified by Lockton Companies, LLC. No wood sheathing replacement is included in this quote and would be an additional cost if required upon site inspection. This modification is intended to provide long-term, low-maintenance protection for the low-slope roof area while improving energy efficiency and reducing environmental impact through a fume-free, quick-installation process.
