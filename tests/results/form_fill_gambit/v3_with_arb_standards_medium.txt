I am proposing to have my flat/low-slope roof replaced using the GAF LIBERTY SBS Self-Adhering Roofing System, installed by American Home Contractors. The project will involve a complete tear-off of the existing roof down to the roof sheathing. A new 1-inch ISO insulation board will be installed and anchored down with screws and plates, tapered as required for proper water drainage. The roofing system itself will be the GAF LIBERTY SBS 2-ply rolled roofing system. This includes the installation of the LIBERTY Self-Adhering Base/Ply Sheet followed by the LIBERTY Self-Adhered Cap Sheet. All seams will be overlapped and all corners and penetrations will be reinforced. New flashings and edge metals will be installed to properly seal the roof perimeter and penetrations, and new pipe flashings will be installed on all vent pipes. The work area will be cleaned and all project-related debris will be disposed of. This system is a self-adhering modified bitumen membrane applied without torches, open flames, hot asphalt, or solvent-based adhesives, which makes for a safer installation with no fumes. The system is backed by GAF and includes a free limited system warranty against material defects for up to 15 years, with optional full-system guarantees available for up to 20 years.
