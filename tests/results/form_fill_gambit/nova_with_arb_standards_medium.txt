The proposed modification involves the replacement of the existing roof with the GAF LIBERTY™ SBS 2-ply Roofing System. This system is designed to provide long-lasting protection for low-slope areas without the need for torches, open flames, hot asphalt, or solvent-based adhesives, making it safer and quicker to install. The LIBERTY™ SBS system features a self-adhering base sheet and cap sheet that overlap all seams and reinforce all corners and penetrations, ensuring a secure and durable installation. The system also includes flashings and edge metals to properly seal the roof perimeter and penetrations, along with new pipe flashings for all vent pipes. The total cost for this project is $7,200, which includes the delivery of materials to the rooftop, tear-off of the existing roof, installation of new 1" ISO insulation board, and clean-up of project-related debris. The GAF LIBERTY™ SBS system is backed by a 15-year limited warranty against material defects and offers optional full-system guarantees against manufacturing defects and workmanship errors for up to 20 years. This roofing system is designed to provide excellent seam strength, long-term weathering, and energy savings due to its reflective and emissive white surface. It is also available in seven popular colors to complement the existing building design.
