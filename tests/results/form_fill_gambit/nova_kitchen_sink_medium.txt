I plan to replace the existing roof on my home located at 12133 Tribune Street, Fairfax, VA, with a new GAF LIBERTY™ SBS 2-Ply Roofing System. The existing roof is in need of replacement due to age and wear, and the new GAF LIBERTY™ SBS system will provide enhanced durability, safety, and energy efficiency. The proposed modification involves the complete removal of the old roofing materials down to the roof sheathing, followed by the installation of a new 1-inch ISO insulation board. The GAF LIBERTY™ SBS Self-Adhering Base/Ply Sheet and GAF LIBERTY™ SBS Self-Adhered Cap Sheet will then be installed, ensuring a secure and weather-resistant roof. The project will also include the installation of all necessary flashings and edge metals to seal the roof perimeter and penetrations, along with new pipe flashings for all vent pipes. The total cost of the project, including materials and labor, is estimated at $7,200.00. This new roofing system offers several benefits, including safer installation without the need for torches, open flames, or solvent-based adhesives, reduced installation time, and a warranty against material defects for up to 15 years. Additionally, the GAF LIBERTY™ SBS system is designed to eliminate fumes and odors associated with traditional roofing methods, providing a healthier and more environmentally friendly option.
