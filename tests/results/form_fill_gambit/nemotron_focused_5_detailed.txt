I will be replacing my flat roof with the GAF LIBERTY SBS Self-Adhered 2-Ply roofing system, which costs $7,200 for the full installation. The roof area to be replaced is 750 square feet. The proposed material is black in color, matching the available color palette for this system. The installation will be completed in one day, with a two-week lead time before work begins. The contractor performing the work is American Home Contractors, LLC, holding Virginia contractor license #2705190396. The scope includes tearing off the existing roof down to the sheathing, furnishing and installing new 1-inch ISO insulation board anchored with screws and plates, installing the LIBERTY SBS Self-Adhering Base/Ply Sheet, applying the GAF LIBERTY SBS Self-Adhered Cap Sheet with all seams overlapped and corners and penetrations reinforced, and installing all flashings and edge metals to properly seal the roof perimeter and penetrations, including new pipe flashings for all vent pipes. All project-related debris will be cleaned and disposed of upon completion. The system is self-adhering, requiring no torches, open flames, hot asphalt, or solvent-based adhesives, and is backed by GAF’s limited warranty against material defects for up to 15 years, with optional extended guarantees available for up to 20 years. The product is FM Approved, UL Classified, Miami-Dade County Product Control Approved, FBC Approved, and Texas Department of Insurance* approved. The installation will follow all applicable standards and manufacturer specifications for proper performance and longevity.
