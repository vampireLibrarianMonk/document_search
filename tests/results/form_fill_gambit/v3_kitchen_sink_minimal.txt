I propose to replace the existing low-slope/flat roof on my property using American Home Contractors, a licensed and insured company with MHIC #31337-03 and Virginia License #27051909396. The work will involve a complete tear-off of the existing roof down to the sheathing. The contractor will then furnish and install new 1-inch ISO insulation board, anchored with screws and plates, to ensure proper water drainage. The new roofing system to be installed is the GAF LIBERTY SBS Self-Adhering 2-ply rolled roofing system. This membrane system is a self-adhering product applied without torches, open flames, hot asphalt, or solvent-based adhesives, thereby eliminating fumes, odors, and on-site fire hazards associated with conventional systems. It is a proven technology backed by a free limited system warranty against material defects for up to 15 years, with options for extended full-system guarantees available. The system will be installed with proper seam overlaps and reinforcement at all corners and penetrations. All flashings and edge metals will be replaced to properly seal the roof perimeter, and new pipe flashings will be installed on all vent pipes. The project includes delivery of materials via boom truck and the cleaning and disposal of all project-related debris. Any necessary replacement of rotted or damaged roof sheathing (plywood) will be addressed as an additional cost, as specified in the proposal.
