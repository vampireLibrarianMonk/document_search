I am planning to replace the existing low-slope flat roof at my property located at 12133 Tribune Street, Fairfax, VA 22033. The proposed modification involves a complete tear-off of the current roofing system down to the roof sheathing, followed by the installation of new 1" ISO insulation board anchored with screws and plates, as needed for proper water drainage. After insulation, I will furnish and install the GAF LIBERTY™ SBS Self-Adhered 2-Ply Roofing System, which includes the base/ply sheet and cap sheet, with all seams overlapped and corners and penetrations reinforced. The system will be completed with new flashings and edge metals to seal the roof perimeter and penetrations, including new pipe flashings for all vent pipes. All project-related debris will be cleaned and disposed of properly.

The materials specified for this project are the GAF LIBERTY™ SBS Self-Adhering Base/Ply Sheet and Cap Sheet, selected in black color to complement the home’s exterior. The total roof area to be covered is approximately 750 square feet. The installation will be performed by American Home Contractors, LLC, who hold Virginia contractor license #2705190396. The project is expected to take one day for installation, with a two-week lead time for material delivery and scheduling. The total cost for the GAF LIBERTY™ SBS Self-Adhered 2-Ply option is $7,200.00, as outlined in the proposal dated May 14, 2026. This system is self-adhering, requires no torches, open flames, hot asphalt, or solvent-based adhesives, and offers a limited warranty against material defects for up to 15 years, with extended guarantees available up to 20 years. The product is FM Approved, UL Classified, Miami-Dade County Product Control Approved, FBC Approved, and available in seven popular colors, including black.
