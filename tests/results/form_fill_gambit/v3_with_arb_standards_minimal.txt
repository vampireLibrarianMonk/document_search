I propose to replace my existing flat roof using American Home Contractors, a licensed Maryland Home Improvement Commission (#31337-03) and Virginia (License #2705190396) contractor. The work involves a complete tear-off of the existing roof down to the sheathing. The contractor will then furnish and install new 1" ISO insulation board, anchored with screws and plates, and tapered if required for proper water drainage. The new roofing system will be the GAF LIBERTY™ SBS Self-Adhered 2-ply Rolled Roofing System. This includes installing the LIBERTY™ Self-Adhering Base/Ply Sheet and the LIBERTY™ Self-Adhered Cap Sheet. The system will be applied without torches, open flames, hot asphalt, or solvent-based adhesives. All seams will be overlapped, and all corners and penetrations will be reinforced. The contractor will also furnish and install all necessary flashings, edge metals, and new pipe flashings to properly seal the roof perimeter and penetrations. All project-related debris will be cleaned and disposed of. The proposal notes that plywood replacement is not included and would be an additional cost if required. The total quoted cost for this scope of work is $7,200.00.
