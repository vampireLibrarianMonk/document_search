Here is some content for the "Description of Proposed Modification" box on the HOA Exterior Modification Application form:

I plan to replace the existing low-slope roof on my home with a GAF LIBERTY SBS Self-Adhered 2-Ply Roofing System. The total roof area is approximately 750 square feet. American Home Contractors will tear off the existing roofing material down to the roof sheathing and install new 1" insulation boards anchored with screws and plates. They will then install GAF's self-adhering LIBERTY SBS Base/Ply Sheet followed by the LIBERTY SBS Self-Adhered Cap Sheet in a black color. All roof seams, corners, and penetrations will be reinforced. New flashings and edge metals will be installed to properly seal the roof perimeter. 

The LIBERTY roofing system does not require torches, open flames, hot asphalt or solvent-based adhesives during installation. It can be installed quickly by a small crew without bulky equipment or unpleasant odors. This project has an approximately 2 week lead time for material delivery and the roof installation is expected to take 1 day. The contractor is licensed in Virginia under license #2705190396. All debris will be cleaned up and properly disposed of upon completion.
