I am proposing to replace my low-slope or flat roof with a new GAF LIBERTY™ SBS Self-Adhering Roofing System as provided by American Home Contractors. This roofing system will be a 2-ply SBS system consisting of a base/ply sheet and a cap sheet. The base/ply sheet is a self-adhering membrane that eliminates the need for torches, open flames, hot asphalt, or solvent-based adhesives. The cap sheet offers enhanced protection and durability with reinforced seams, corners, and penetrations. The cap sheet color will be selected at a later date. This system provides a safer, cleaner, and more efficient installation method with minimal setup and cleanup time. It is also backed by a manufacturer's warranty and is FM Approved, UL Classified, and meets various other performance and safety standards. I understand that this project includes tearing off my existing roof to the sheathing, installing new insulation, and completing necessary flashing and edge metal work to ensure proper sealing and performance of the new roofing system.
