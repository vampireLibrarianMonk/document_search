I am proposing to replace the low-slope/flat roof on my property with a GAF EverGuard® TPO roofing system as provided by American Home Contractors. The new roof will include the removal of the existing roofing down to the sheathing, installation of new 1" ISO insulation board anchored to the roof, and the application of GAF EverGuard® TPO 60-mil black membrane. The TPO membrane will be installed in a mechanically fastened method with heat-welded seams and reinforced in all corners and penetrations. All flashings and edge metal will be properly sealed to ensure a watertight roof system. The contractor will also install new pipe flashings for all vent pipes and clean up and dispose of all debris. The selected roof color is black. American Home Contractors will handle the delivery of materials and project management, including the use of a boom truck for transporting materials to the rooftop.
