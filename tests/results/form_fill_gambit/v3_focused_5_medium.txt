I propose to replace my existing flat roof using the GAF LIBERTY SBS (Styrene-Butadiene-Styrene) Self-Adhering Roofing System, installed by American Home Contractors. The LIBERTY system is a self-adhering, two-ply modified bitumen membrane. The installation will be performed without the use of torches, open flames, hot asphalt, or solvent-based adhesives, which eliminates associated fumes, odors, and on-site fire risks. This method is safer and allows for a quicker installation, often one-third faster than conventional systems, with minimal set-up and clean-up time and no need for staging expensive equipment.

The work will involve a complete tear-off of the existing roof down to the roof sheathing. New one-inch ISO insulation board will be furnished and installed, anchored with screws and plates, and tapered if required for proper water drainage. The roofing system will consist of the GAF LIBERTY Self-Adhering Base/Ply Sheet overlapped with the GAF LIBERTY Self-Adhered Cap Sheet. All seams will be overlapped and all corners and penetrations will be reinforced. All flashings and edge metals will be furnished and installed to properly seal the roof perimeter and penetrations, including new pipe flashings for all vent pipes. The system comes with a free limited system warranty against material defects for up to 15 years from GAF, with optional full-system guarantees against manufacturing defects and workmanship errors available for up to 20 years. The LIBERTY system carries several applicable standards approvals including FM Approved and UL Classified.

I have selected this GAF LIBERTY SBS system for its proven technology, based on the same modified bitumen technology used in GAF's commercial roofing systems, and because it is backed by GAF, North America's largest roofing manufacturer. The system has also earned the Good Housekeeping Seal. The contractor, American Home Contractors, is a licensed GAF Master Elite contractor (MHIC #31337-03, VA License #2705190396). All project-related debris will be cleaned and disposed of upon completion.
