I am proposing to replace my low-slope/flat roof with a GAF EverGuard® TPO roofing system. American Home Contractors will complete this project by tearing off the existing roof down to the sheathing, installing new 1" ISO insulation board to ensure proper water drainage, and applying a 60-mil, black EverGuard® TPO roofing membrane using a mechanically fastened method. The project will also include installing all required flashings and edge metals to seal the roof perimeter, replacing pipe flashings on all vent pipes, and ensuring clean disposal of all debris. This system provides excellent durability, energy efficiency through its reflective surface, and is backed by GAF's comprehensive warranty.
