I am proposing to replace my flat roof using the GAF LIBERTY SBS Self-Adhered 2-Ply Roofing System. The project will cover 750 square feet of roof area and will be completed in black, matching the color option specified in the proposal. The work will include tearing off the existing roof down to the sheathing, furnishing and installing new 1" ISO insulation board anchored with screws and plates, applying the LIBERTY SBS Self-Adhering Base/Ply Sheet and Cap Sheet with all seams overlapped and corners and penetrations reinforced, installing flashings and edge metals to seal the roof perimeter, adding new pipe flashings to all vent pipes, and cleaning and disposing of all project debris. The installation will be performed by American Home Contractors, who hold Virginia license #2705190396, and is expected to take one day to complete. The materials will be delivered with a two-week lead time, and the total cost for this scope of work is $7,200.00 as outlined in the proposal dated May 14, 2026. This system is self-adhering, requiring no torches, open flames, hot asphalt, or solvent-based adhesives, ensuring a safer and cleaner installation process.
