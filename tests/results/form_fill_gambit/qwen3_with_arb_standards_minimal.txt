I am having the low-slope/flat roof of my property replaced by American Home Contractors (M.H.I.C. #31337-03, VA License #27051909396). The proposed roofing system is the GAF Everguard® TPO Roofing System, specifically a 60-mil, black-colored membrane. This system is a thermoplastic polyolefin (TPO) roofing membrane that will be mechanically fastened to the roof surface. The installation includes the tear-off of the existing roof down to the sheathing, the installation of new 1" ISO insulation board anchored with screws and plates to facilitate proper water drainage, and the furnishing and installation of the GAF Everguard® TPO membrane with heat-welded seams, reinforced at all corners and penetrations.

In addition, all flashing and edge metals will be installed to properly seal the roof perimeter and penetrations, and new pipe flashings will be installed for all vent pipes. The proposal includes the cleaning and disposal of all project-related debris. This roofing system is selected for its excellent durability, energy-saving properties, and long-term weather resistance. The TPO membrane is a highly reflective, white surface that helps reduce energy costs and is inherently flexible and naturally fungal resistant without the need for biocides. The system comes with a limited warranty and is backed by GAF, North America’s largest roofing manufacturer.
