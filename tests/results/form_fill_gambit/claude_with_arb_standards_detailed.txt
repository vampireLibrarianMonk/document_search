Here is the content for the "Description of Proposed Modification" box on the HOA Exterior Modification Application form:

I plan to replace my existing flat roof with a new GAF LIBERTY SBS Self-Adhered 2-Ply roofing system. The total roof area is approximately 750 square feet. The new roofing will be installed in a black color. 

The work will be performed by American Home Contractors, a licensed contractor in Virginia (license #2705190396). They will tear off the existing roof down to the roof sheathing and install 1" insulation board anchored with screws and plates. Then they will install the self-adhering LIBERTY SBS base/ply sheet and cap sheet, overlapping and reinforcing all seams, corners and penetrations. New flashings and edge metals will be installed to properly seal the roof perimeter. 

The installation process does not require any torches, open flames, hot asphalt or solvent-based adhesives, eliminating fumes and fire hazards. It is a 2-ply system that comes with a manufacturer's warranty against material defects for up to 15 years. The installation crew is expected to be 2-3 workers and the roof replacement should take approximately 1 day to complete once materials are delivered. Lead time for material delivery is around 2 weeks.
