I am proposing to install the GAF LIBERTY SBS self-adhering roofing system on the low-slope areas of your roof. This system eliminates the need for open flames, hot asphalt or solvent-based adhesives during installation. The base/ply sheets and cap sheet membrane are self-adhering and can be installed quickly without bulky equipment or unpleasant fumes. 

The LIBERTY system uses proven modified bitumen technology that GAF has used for decades in their commercial roofing products. It comes with a limited material warranty of up to 15 years, with optional full system guarantees against manufacturing defects and workmanship errors available for up to 20 years.

The LIBERTY cap sheet is available in 7 popular colors - Black, Hickory, Shakewood, Slate, Tan, Weathered Wood, and White - to complement your main roof shingles. The system meets FM, UL, Miami-Dade and other code approvals for fire, wind and impact resistance.

Installation involves adhering the self-stick base/ply sheets, followed by the granulated cap sheet membrane, with all seams overlapped and reinforced at corners and penetrations. New insulation, flashings and edging would also be installed for a complete warranted roof system.
