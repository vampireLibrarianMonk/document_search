I plan to have the existing flat roof replaced using the GAF LIBERTY SBS Self-Adhered 2-Ply Roofing System. The project will cover 750 square feet of roof area, and I have selected the black color option for the cap sheet to match the aesthetic of my home. The installation will be performed by American Home Contractors, who hold Virginia contractor license #2705190396. The work includes a site visit, delivery of materials by boom truck, tear-off of the existing roof down to the sheathing, installation of 1" ISO insulation board anchored with screws and plates, and the application of both the LIBERTY SBS Self-Adhering Base/Ply Sheet and Cap Sheet. All seams will be overlapped, and all corners and penetrations will be reinforced. Flashings and edge metals will be installed to seal the roof perimeter and vent pipes, and all debris will be cleaned and disposed of upon completion. The total cost for this scope of work is $7,200. The installation is expected to take one day, with a two-week lead time from authorization to start date. No plywood sheathing replacement is included in this quote, as it would incur additional costs if needed. All materials are new and self-adhering, requiring no torches, open flames, hot asphalt, or solvent-based adhesives during installation.
