I am planning to replace the flat roof on my house with American Home Contractors. The proposed replacement will use the GAF Liberty™ SBS Self-Adhering Roofing System. This system is applied without torches, open flames, hot asphalt, or messy solvent-based adhesives, which makes it a much safer and cleaner installation. It has a free limited system warranty against material defects for up to 15 years and optional full-system guarantees against manufacturing defects and workmanship errors available for up to 20 years. Additionally, it's backed by GAF, North America's largest roofing manufacturer, and comes in several colors to complement the rest of the house. This roofing system is FM Approved, UL Classified, Miami-Dade County Product Control Approved, and FBC Approved, among other standards. It features a self-adhering base/ply sheet and a cap sheet, with specified roll lengths, widths, thicknesses, and weights. The total project will cover approximately 1216 square feet (12 squares).
