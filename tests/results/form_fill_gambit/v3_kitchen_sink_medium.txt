I propose to replace my existing flat/low-slope roof with the GAF LIBERTY SBS Self-Adhering Roofing System, installed by American Home Contractors. This is a two-ply modified bitumen system. The work will involve a complete tear-off of the existing roof down to the sheathing. A new 1-inch ISO insulation board will be furnished and installed, anchored with screws and plates, and tapered if required for proper drainage. The system features a self-adhering base/ply sheet topped with a self-adhering cap sheet. All seams will be overlapped, and all corners and penetrations will be reinforced. New flashings, edge metals, and pipe flashings will be installed to properly seal the roof perimeter and penetrations. This system is applied without torches, open flames, hot asphalt, or solvent-based adhesives, making it a safer and cleaner installation process with no fumes. It is backed by GAF and carries material warranties. The project includes the delivery of materials, all labor, and the cleanup and disposal of all project-related debris.
