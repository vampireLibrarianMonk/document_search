Here is the proposed description of the exterior modification for the HOA application form:

I propose to replace the existing flat roof on my home with a new GAF LIBERTY SBS Self-Adhering 2-ply rolled roofing system in the color [insert color selection from options]. This is a torch-free, low odor application that eliminates the need for open flames on the roof. The roofing system includes a self-adhering base/ply sheet and self-adhering cap sheet that are applied without hot asphalt or solvent-based adhesives. It provides long-lasting protection backed by GAF, North America's largest roofing manufacturer. All seams will be overlapped and reinforced, and new flashings and edge metals will be installed to properly seal the roof perimeter and penetrations. The existing roof will be torn off down to the roof sheathing and new 1" insulation board will be installed prior to the new roofing membrane.
