I am proposing to install a new low-slope/flat roof using the GAF LIBERTY SBS self-adhering roofing system. This is a two-ply system consisting of a self-adhering base/ply sheet and a self-adhering cap sheet. The roofing membrane is applied without the need for torches, open flames, hot asphalt or messy solvent-based adhesives. 

The base sheet and cap sheet roll out and adhere directly to the roof deck or insulation using the system's self-adhesive properties. All seams are overlapped and the corners and penetrations are reinforced. The GAF LIBERTY system eliminates fumes, odors and fire risks associated with conventionally applied low-slope roof systems. It is a proven technology based on GAF's commercial modified bitumen roofing systems that have been used for decades.

The LIBERTY cap sheet will be installed in the [Color] color to complement the existing shingle roof. Upon completion, new metal flashings and edge details will be installed to properly terminate the roof system at walls and penetrations. The LIBERTY roofing system meets applicable standards including FM Approval, UL Classification, Miami-Dade County Product Control Approval, Florida Building Code Approval, and acceptance under the Texas Department of Insurance program.
