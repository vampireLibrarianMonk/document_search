We are replacing our flat roof with a low-slope roof using the GAF LIBERTY™ SBS 2-Ply Roofing System. The project will be done by American Home Contractors, LLC, a licensed and insured roofing company. The scope of work includes delivering materials to the rooftop, tearing off the existing roof down to the roof sheathing, furnishing and installing new insulation and roofing materials, including the base sheet and crew to rooftop, tearing off the existing roof, installing new ISO insulation board anchored with screws and plates, furnace wall, and installing the GAF LIBERTY™ SBS Self-Adhering Base/Ply Sheet and Cap Sheet in the color of our choice, which will be determined before the work commences. The job will also include furnishing all flashings and edge metals to properly seal the roof perimeter and penetrations, installing new pipe flashings to all vent pipes, and cleaning and disposing of all project-related debris, this scope of work  is valued at $7,200.00. The materials will be installed by a team led by the roof project supervisor, who will ensure adherence to the highest standards of workmanship using only the specified materials. American Home Contractors is fully licensed, insured, and dedicated to providing top-quality exterior modifications.
