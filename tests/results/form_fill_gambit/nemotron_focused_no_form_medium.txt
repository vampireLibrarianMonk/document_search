I am proposing to replace my flat roof using the American Home Contractors GAF LIBERTY SBS self-adhering roofing system. This system is a torch-free, low-slope roofing solution that eliminates the need for open flames, hot asphalt, or solvent-based adhesives during installation. The LIBERTY™ SBS system consists of a self-adhering base/ply sheet and a self-adhering cap sheet, both made with SBS-modified bitumen technology proven in GAF’s commercial roofing applications for decades. The base sheet is 0.068 inches thick, 39.375 inches wide, and 66 feet long, covering 216 square feet per roll. The cap sheet is 0.157 inches thick, same width, and 34.0 feet long, covering 112 square feet per roll. The system is applied without torches, kettles, or special tools, allowing for quicker installation—often one-third faster than conventional methods—with minimal setup and cleanup. It is FM approved, UL classified, Miami-Dade County product control approved, FBC approved, and features a limited warranty against material defects for up to 15 years, with optional full-system guarantees available for up to 20 years. The installation includes tear-off of the existing roof down to sheathing, furnishing and installing new 1-inch ISO insulation board anchored with screws and plates (tapered if needed for drainage), installing the LIBERTY™ SBS self-adhering base/ply sheet, followed by the self-adhering cap sheet with all seams overlapped and corners and penetrations reinforced. All flashings and edge metals will be installed to seal the roof perimeter and penetrations, new pipe flashings will be added to all vent pipes, and all project-related debris will be cleaned and disposed of. The color selection for the cap sheet will be chosen from the GAF LIBERTY™ SBS color palette—Black, Hickory, Shakewood, Slate, Tan, Weathered Wood, or White—to complement the existing residential shingle roof. This system provides a safe, clean, durable, and long-lasting solution for my low-slope roof area with proven technology and strong manufacturer backing from GAF, North America’s largest roofing manufacturer.
