I propose to replace the existing flat roof on my property. The project will be completed by American Home Contractors, a licensed and insured contractor. The work will involve the complete tear-off of the existing roof down to the sheathing. A new 1-inch ISO insulation board will be installed, anchored with screws and plates, and tapered as required for proper water drainage. The roofing membrane to be installed is the GAF EverGuard TPO (Thermoplastic Polyolefin) system, specifically a 60-mil, mechanically fastened membrane in the color black. All seams will be heat-welded, and all corners and penetrations will be reinforced. The project includes the furnishing and installation of all necessary flashings, edge metals, and new pipe flashings to properly seal the roof perimeter and penetrations. All project-related debris will be cleaned and disposed of upon completion.
