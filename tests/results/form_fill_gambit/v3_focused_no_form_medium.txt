I propose to replace the existing flat roof on my property. The work will be performed by American Home Contractors, a licensed contractor with MHIC #31337-03 and VA License #2705190396. The proposed system is the GAF LIBERTY SBS Self-Adhering Roofing System. This is a two-ply modified bitumen system. The installation will involve a complete tear-off of the existing roof down to the roof sheathing. New 1-inch ISO insulation board will be furnished and installed, anchored with screws and plates, and tapered if required for proper water drainage. The contractor will then furnish and install the LIBERTY SBS Self-Adhering Base/Ply Sheet, followed by the GAF LIBERTY SBS Self-Adhered Cap Sheet. All seams will be overlapped and all corners and penetrations will be reinforced. New flashings and edge metals will be installed to properly seal the roof perimeter and penetrations, and new pipe flashings will be installed on all vent pipes. The system will be applied without torches, open flames, hot asphalt, or solvent-based adhesives, which eliminates associated fumes and odors. The LIBERTY system carries a free limited system warranty against material defects for up to 15 years, with optional full-system guarantees available for up to 20 years. The work will include the delivery of materials, installation, and the cleanup and disposal of all project-related debris. Please note that the replacement or installation of new roof sheathing, if required, is an additional cost not included in the base proposal.
