The proposed modification involves the replacement of the existing flat roof with a new GAF LIBERTY™ SBS 2-ply Roofing System. The project will include the removal of the existing roof down to the sheathing, installation of new 1" ISO insulation board, and installation of the LIBERTY™ SBS Self-Adhering Base/Ply Sheet and GAF LIBERTY™ SBS Self-Adhering Cap Sheet. Additionally, all flashings, edge metals, and pipe flashings will be installed to seal the roof perimeter and penetrations. The total cost for this project is $7,200.00.
