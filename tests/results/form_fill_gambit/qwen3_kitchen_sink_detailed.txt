I am proposing to replace the flat roof of my property located at 12133 Tribune Street, Fairfax, VA, with a GAF LIBERTY SBS Self-Adhered 2-Ply roofing system. This modification involves tearing off the existing roof down to the sheathing, installing new 1" ISO insulation board, and applying two layers of the GAF LIBERTY™ SBS Self-Adhering roofing system. The installation will use a black-colored cap sheet, which will be overlapped at all seams and reinforced around all corners and penetrations. I have selected the SBS Self-Adhered 2-ply option as it is an approved application that eliminates the use of torches, open flames, and solvent-based adhesives. The roof area being modified is 750 square feet and is expected to be completed in one day with a lead time of two weeks. This project is estimated to cost $7,200, and I have retained American Home Contractors, a licensed Virginia contractor with license number #2705190396, to carry out the work.
