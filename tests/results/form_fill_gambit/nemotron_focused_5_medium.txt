I am proposing to replace my existing flat roof with the GAF LIBERTY SBS Self-Adhering 2-ply Roofing System, a torch-free, low-slope roofing solution that eliminates the need for open flames, hot asphalt, or solvent-based adhesives during installation. This system consists of a self-adhering base/ply sheet and a self-adhering cap sheet, both manufactured by GAF, North America’s largest roofing manufacturer. The base sheet measures 66 feet in length, 39.375 inches in width, and 0.068 inches in thickness, weighing 82 pounds per roll and covering 216 square feet (2 squares). The cap sheet is 34.0 feet long, same width, 0.157 inches thick, weighs 96.4 pounds per roll, and covers 112 square feet (1 square). The system is FM Approved, UL Classified, Miami-Dade County Product Control Approved, FBC Approved, and Texas Department of Insurance approved (where applicable). It utilizes proven technology derived from GAF’s commercial modified bitumen roofing systems, offering a limited warranty against material defects for up to 15 years, with optional full-system guarantees extending up to 20 years for manufacturing defects and workmanship errors. The installation process includes tearing off the existing roof down to the sheathing, furnishing and installing new 1-inch ISO insulation board anchored with screws and plates (tapered as needed for drainage), applying the Liberty SBS base/ply sheet, installing the cap sheet with overlapped seams and reinforced corners and penetrations, furnishing and installing all flashings and edge metals to seal the roof perimeter and penetrations, installing new pipe flashings on all vent pipes, and thoroughly cleaning and disposing of all project-related debris. The system is available in seven popular colors—Black, Hickory, Shakewood, Slate, Tan, Weathered Wood, and White—to complement existing residential shingle roofs. This method ensures safer working conditions for installers by eliminating torches, kettles, and hazardous chemicals, reduces installation time by approximately one-third compared to conventional systems, requires no special tools, and minimizes disruption, fumes, and odors during application. I have selected this system for its durability, ease of installation, safety benefits, and long-term performance backed by GAF’s industry-leading warranty and technical support.
