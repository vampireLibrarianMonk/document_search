The proposed modification for my property at 12133 Tribune Street, Fairfax, VA, involves the replacement of the existing low-slope/flat roof with a new GAF LIBERTY™ SBS Self-Adhering 2-ply Roofing System. This project is being proposed by American Home Contractors, led by Patrick Flanigan, who can be contacted at pmflani@gmail.com or (540) 209-0256. The scope of work includes tearing off the existing roof down to the roof sheathing, installing new 1" ISO insulation board, and furnishing & installing the new roofing system, including the LIBERTY™ SBS Self-Adhering Base/Ply Sheet and LIBERTY™ SBS Self-Adhering Cap Sheet. The project will also involve the installation of flashings, edge metals, and pipe flashings to seal the roof perimeter and penetrations. Additionally, all project-related debris will be cleaned and disposed of. The proposed roofing system is designed to provide long-lasting protection for the low-slope areas of the property without the danger or hassles associated with the installation of other conventional roofing products, and it is applied without the use of torches, open flames, hot asphalt, or solvent-based adhesives. This modification is intended to enhance the durability and aesthetic appeal of my property while ensuring compliance with local building and zoning code requirements.
