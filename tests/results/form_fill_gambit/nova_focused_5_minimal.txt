I am planning to replace the existing flat roof on my home located at 12133 Tribune Street, Fairfax, VA, with a new low-slope/flat roof using a GAF LIBERTY™ SBS 2-ply Roofing System. The proposed modification includes the complete removal of the existing roof down to the roof sheathing, installation of new 1" ISO insulation board, and the application of the GAF LIBERTY™ SBS Self-Adhering Base/Ply Sheet and Cap Sheet. Additionally, all flashings, edge metals, and pipe flashings will be installed to ensure proper sealing of the roof perimeter and penetrations. The project also involves cleaning and disposing of all project-related debris. The total estimated cost for this project is $7,200.00.
