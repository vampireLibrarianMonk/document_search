I am planning to modify the exterior of my property, specifically the roof. I have received a proposal from American Home Contractors for a flat roof replacement. The proposal is for a low-slope/flat roof replacement using a self-adhering roofing system. This system is applied without torches, open flames, hot asphalt, or messy solvent-based adhesives, making it safer and quicker to install compared to traditional roofing systems. It is based on the same technology used in GAF's commercial modified bitumen roofing systems, which have been proven in the field for decades. The proposal includes a free limited system warranty against material defects for up to 15 years, with optional full-system guarantees against manufacturing defects and workmanship errors available for up to 20 years. The roofing system is also backed by GAF, North America's largest roofing manufacturer. There are seven popular colors to choose from to complement my existing shingle roof. The proposal also mentions that the system has been approved by FM, UL, Miami-Dade County Product Control, and the Texas Department of Insurance.
