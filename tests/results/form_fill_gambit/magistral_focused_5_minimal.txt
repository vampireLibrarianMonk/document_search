We plan to proceed with the GAF Everguard TPO Roofing System as outlined in the proposal from American Home Contractors. This system will replace our current flat roof, providing better long-term weathering, energy savings, and a highly reflective and emissive white surface to help reduce energy costs. The proposal includes a complete tear-off of the existing roof down to the roof sheathing, installation of new 1" ISO insulation board, and the application of the GAF Everguard 60-mil TPO Mechanically Fastened Roofing Membrane in Black. Additionally, the proposal covers the installation of all necessary flashings, edge metals, and pipe flashings to properly seal the roof perimeter and penetrations. The total price for this work is $17,400.00, not including the cost of plywood replacement, which will be an additional expense if needed. We understand that we are required to show compliance with our Homeowner's Association regulations, and this project has received the necessary approval, which is included with this application.
