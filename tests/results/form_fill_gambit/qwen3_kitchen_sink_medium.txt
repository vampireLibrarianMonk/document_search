I propose to replace the low-slope/flat roof on my property using the GAF LIBERTY SBS Self-Adhering Roofing System. This system will be installed without the use of torches, open flames, hot asphalt, or messy solvent-based adhesives, making it a safer and more environmentally-friendly option. The base sheet and cap sheet of the system offer a thickness of 0.068 inches and 0.157 inches, respectively, providing durable protection for the roofing area. The selected color for the cap sheet will complement the exterior of my property while also contributing to energy efficiency benefits due to the highly reflective surface. This roofing system features self-adhering technology, quick installation with minimal cleanup, and eliminates fumes and odors typically associated with traditional roofing methods. The roofing material complies with various industry standards, including FM Approval, UL Classification, Miami-Dade County Product Control Approval, and FBC approval, ensuring quality and safety. This project will include the removal and replacement of existing roofing material, the installation of new roof insulation, base and cap sheets, and all necessary flashings and edge metals to ensure a fully functional and weatherproof roof system.
