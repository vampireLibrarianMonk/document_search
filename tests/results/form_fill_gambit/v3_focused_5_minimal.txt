I propose to replace the existing flat/low-slope roof on my property. The work will be performed by American Home Contractors, LLC. The scope includes a complete tear-off of the existing roof down to the roof sheathing. The contractor will then furnish and install new 1" ISO insulation board, anchored with screws and plates, tapered if required for proper water drainage. The new roofing system will be a GAF Everguard® TPO (Thermoplastic Polyolefin) Mechanically Fastened Roofing Membrane. The membrane will be 60-mil thickness and colored black. All seams will be heat-welded, and all corners and penetrations will be reinforced. The contractor will furnish and install all necessary flashings and edge metals to properly seal the roof perimeter and penetrations, as well as new pipe flashings for all vent pipes. The project includes delivery of materials via boom truck, and all project-related debris will be cleaned and disposed of. The existing roof sheathing will be inspected; replacement of any rotted or damaged plywood sheathing is not included in the base quote and would be an additional cost. The contractor is licensed (MHIC #31337-03, VA License #2705190396) and insured.
