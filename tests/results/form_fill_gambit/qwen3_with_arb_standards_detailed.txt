I am proposing to replace the flat roof on my home located at 12133 Tribune Street, Fairfax, VA, using the GAF LIBERTY™ SBS Self-Adhered 2-Ply Roofing System. The contractor for this project is American Home Contractors, who is licensed in Virginia under #2705190396. The total project cost is $7,200 and will cover a roof area of 750 square feet. The selected color for the roofing system is black. The installation will take one day to complete and requires a lead time of approximately two weeks from the ordering date. The materials are self-adhering and do not require torches, open flames, or solvent-based adhesives, making the application safer and more environmentally friendly. The system also eliminates the fumes and odors commonly associated with traditional roofing methods. This roofing solution is backed by GAF and includes a limited warranty against material defects for up to 15 years, with optional extended guarantees available. Installation involves tearing off the existing roof down to the sheathing, installing new 1" ISO insulation board, and applying the two-ply self-adhered roofing system with proper flashing and edge metal to ensure a watertight seal. The system will overlap all seams, and the corners and penetrations will be reinforced to provide added durability and protection.
