I will be replacing the existing flat roofing system at my home located at 12133 Tribune Street, Fairfax, VA 22033, with the GAF LIBERTY™ SBS Self-Adhering Roofing System. This system is a two-ply modified bitumen roofing solution designed for low-slope applications. The installation will involve removing the current roofing material down to the roof sheathing, furnishing and installing new 1-inch ISO insulation board anchored with screws and plates (tapered as needed for proper drainage), followed by the application of the GAF LIBERTY™ SBS Self-Adhering Base/Ply Sheet and Cap Sheet. All seams will be overlapped, and all corners and penetrations will be reinforced. The system will be completed with new flashings and edge metals to seal the roof perimeter and all vent pipes. The LIBERTY™ system is applied without torches, open flames, hot asphalt, or solvent-based adhesives, making it a clean, safe, and efficient installation. It features proven technology based on GAF’s commercial modified bitumen systems, offers up to a 15-year limited warranty against material defects, and is available in seven popular colors to complement the home’s exterior. The system is FM Approved, UL Classified, Miami-Dade County Product Control Approved, FBC Approved, and Texas Department of Insurance recognized. All work will be performed by American Home Contractors, a licensed Virginia contractor (VA License #27051909396, M.H.I.C. #31337-03), in full compliance with local building codes and permit requirements.
