I am proposing to replace the low-slope/flat roof at 12133 Tribune Street, Fairfax, VA 22033 using the GAF LIBERTY™ SBS Self-Adhering 2-ply Roofing System. This system includes furnishing and installing new 1" ISO insulation board anchored with screws and plates, the LIBERTY™ SBS Self-Adhering Base/Ply Sheet, and the GAF LIBERTY™ SBS Self-Adhering Cap Sheet. All seams will be overlapped, and all corners and penetrations will be reinforced. The system will be installed without torches, open flames, hot asphalt, or messy solvent-based adhesives, ensuring a safer, cleaner installation with minimal disruption. The LIBERTY™ SBS system is based on proven technology from GAF’s commercial modified bitumen roofing systems, offers a free limited warranty against material defects for up to 15 years, and optional full-system guarantees for up to 20 years. It is FM Approved, UL Classified, Miami-Dade County Product Control Approved, FBC Approved, and designed to provide long-lasting protection for low-slope areas while eliminating fumes, odors, and the need for open flames during installation. The color of the cap sheet will be selected to complement the existing residential shingle roof, choosing from the seven popular colors available: Black, Hickory, Shakewood, Slate, Tan, Weathered Wood, or White. All flashings, edge metals, and pipe flashings will be furnished and installed to properly seal the roof perimeter and penetrations, and all project-related debris will be cleaned and disposed of upon completion. The total cost for this scope of work is $7,200.00, which includes delivery of materials by boom truck, tear-off of the existing roof down to sheathing, and installation of all components as specified. Plywood sheathing replacement is not included in this quote and would be an additional cost if required. All materials will be new, and the work will be performed in a good workmanlike manner in compliance with all local requirements for building permits, inspections, and zoning.
