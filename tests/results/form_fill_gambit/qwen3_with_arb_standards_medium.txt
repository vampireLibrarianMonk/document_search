I am proposing to replace the low-slope/flat roof at my residence with a GAF Liberty SBS Self-Adhering Roofing System. This system offers a safe and efficient alternative to traditional roofing methods as it utilizes self-adhering technology that eliminates the use of torches, open flames, hot asphalt, or solvent-based adhesives during installation. This provides a safer environment for both the roofing crew and the property. The system also eliminates fumes and odors typically associated with conventional applications.

The proposed system is based on the same proven technology used in GAF's commercial modified bitumen roofing systems, ensuring long-term performance and reliability. The application of the Liberty SBS system ensures quicker installation times compared to traditional methods. It is also compatible with various roof types and requires minimal equipment. Additionally, the system comes with a limited warranty of up to 15 years and offers the option of an extended guarantee for up to 20 years. The Liberty SBS system is available in seven colors, which will be selected to complement the existing roofing on my home.

This self-adhering roofing system meets various industry standards including FM Approval, UL Classification, Miami-Dade County Product Control Approval, and FBC Approval. It includes a base sheet, self-adhering base/ply sheet, and a cap sheet, with specific dimensions and weights to ensure proper coverage and durability. The selected system will be installed by American Home Contractors, a licensed and insured roofing contractor with experience utilizing the Liberty SBS system. The contractor will ensure all procedures, including flashing, sealing, and proper overlap of seams, are completed according to GAF specifications to maintain the warranty and performance of the roofing system.
