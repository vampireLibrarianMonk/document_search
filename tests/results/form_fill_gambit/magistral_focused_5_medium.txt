I am replacing the existing low-slope roof with the GAF LIBERTY™ SBS 2-PLY ROOFING SYSTEM. The system will be installed without the use of torches, open flames, or messy solvent-based adhesives, making it a safer option for my building. The LIBERTY™ SBS Self-Adhering Roofing System consists of a LIBERTY™ MA Base Sheet and a LIBERTY™ Self-Adhering Cap Sheet. The base sheet will be 2 squares, 216 sq ft with a roll length of 66 ft and width of 39.375 in. The cap sheet will be 1 square, 112 sq ft with a roll length of 34.0 ft and width of 39.375 in. Both sheets are designed to complement the existing shingles, with color options including White, Black, Tan, Slate, Weathered Wood, Hickory, and Shakewood. The installation will also include new ISO insulation board anchored down with screws, all flashings and edge metals to properly seal the roof perimeter and penetrations, and new pipe flashings for all vent pipes. Additionally, a site visit will be conducted by the roof project supervisor to verify exact materials needed, and all project-related debris will be cleaned and disposed of. The chosen roof color is Tan. The roof system has a 15-year limited warranty, with extended guarantees available up to 20 years.
