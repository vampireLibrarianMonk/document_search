The proposed modification is a complete replacement of the low-slope/flat roof on my home. I have selected the GAF LIBERTY SBS Self-Adhered 2-Ply Roofing System from American Home Contractors for this project. The work will involve tearing off the existing roof down to the roof sheathing. The contractor will then furnish and install new 1-inch ISO insulation board, anchored with screws and plates, which will be tapered if required for proper water drainage. Following that, they will furnish and install the LIBERTY Self-Adhering Base/Ply Sheet and then the GAF LIBERTY SBS Self-Adhered Cap Sheet in the color black. All seams will be overlapped, and all corners and penetrations will be reinforced. The project includes furnishing and installing all necessary flashings, edge metals, and new pipe flashings to properly seal the roof perimeter and penetrations. Upon completion, all project-related debris will be cleaned and disposed of.

The roofing area to be addressed is approximately 750 square feet, as detailed in the provided proposal. The materials are the GAF LIBERTY system, a self-adhering membrane applied without torches, open flames, hot asphalt, or solvent-based adhesives. The installation is scheduled to be completed in one day. The contractor has indicated a lead time of approximately two weeks from signed authorization to schedule the work.
