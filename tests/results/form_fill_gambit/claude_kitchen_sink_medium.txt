I propose the following for the description of proposed modification section:

I am requesting approval to replace the low-slope roofing on my home with a GAF LIBERTY SBS Self-Adhering Roofing System. This is a 2-ply self-adhesive rolled roofing system that can be installed without open flames, hot asphalt or solvent-based adhesives. 

The existing roof will be torn off down to the roof sheathing. New 1" insulation board will be installed and anchored to the roof deck. Then the LIBERTY SBS Base/Ply sheet will be adhered followed by the LIBERTY SBS Cap sheet in the selected color. All seams will be overlapped and reinforced. New flashings and edge metals will be installed to properly seal the roof perimeter and penetrations. Upon completion, the roofing system will have a 15 year limited manufacturer warranty against material defects.

This low-odor system eliminates open flames, fumes, and requires no special tools for installation. It is based on the same proven technology as GAF's commercial modified bitumen roofing that has performed well for decades. The self-adhesive application is often faster than conventional systems with less setup and cleanup required. As North America's largest roofing manufacturer, GAF stands behind this product.
