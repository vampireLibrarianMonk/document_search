I am proposing to replace the low-slope/flat roof on my property with a GAF Liberty™ SBS Self-Adhering Roofing System. This system is a two-ply rolled roofing solution that eliminates the need for torches, open flames, hot asphalt, or messy solvent-based adhesives, making the installation process safer for both the workers and my home. The system consists of a base/ply sheet and a self-adhering cap sheet, with the cap sheet being overlapped and reinforced at all corners and penetrations to ensure watertight integrity. The project will also include a new 1" ISO insulation board, anchored down with screws and plates, and all required flashings and edge metals to properly seal the roof perimeter and penetrations. The selected color for the cap sheet is yet to be finalized. The roofing contractor will tear off the existing roof down to the sheathing and deliver all materials to the rooftop using a boom truck. Debris from the project will be cleaned and disposed of upon completion. This roofing system provides a 15-year limited warranty against material defects with the option for extended guarantees up to 20 years.
