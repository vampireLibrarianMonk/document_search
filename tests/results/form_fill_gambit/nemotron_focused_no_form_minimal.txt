We propose to replace the existing flat roof at 12133 Tribune Street, Fairfax, VA 22033 with a GAF Liberty™ SBS Self-Adhering 2-ply Roofing System. This includes tearing off the existing roof down to the sheathing, furnishing and installing new 1" ISO insulation board anchored with screws and plates (tapered if required for proper drainage), installing the Liberty™ SBS Self-Adhering Base/Ply Sheet, installing the GAF Liberty™ SBS Self-Adhered Cap Sheet in a selected color to complement the home, overlapping all seams, reinforcing all corners and penetrations, furnishing and installing all flashings and edge metals to seal the roof perimeter and penetrations, installing new pipe flashings on all vent pipes, and cleaning and disposing of all project-related debris. The system is applied without torches, open flames, hot asphalt, or messy solvent-based adhesives, providing a safer, cleaner installation with proven technology based on GAF’s commercial modified bitumen roofing systems. It offers a free limited system warranty against material defects for up to 15 years, with optional full-system guarantees available for up to 20 years. The product is FM Approved, UL Classified, Miami-Dade County Product Control Approved, FBC Approved, and Texas Department of Insurance approved. The proposed work will be performed by American Home Contractors, LLC, licensed in Virginia (License #2705190396) and holding M.H.I.C. #31337-03. All materials will be new unless otherwise specified. The total cost for this scope of work is $7,200.00, with additional costs possible for plywood sheathing replacement or HVAC relocation if needed, to be quoted upon final site inspection. The homeowner is responsible for confirming color selection, and the contractor will remove all debris and leave work areas in broom clean condition upon completion. This modification is intended to provide long-lasting protection for the low-slope roof area while enhancing safety, durability, and energy efficiency.
