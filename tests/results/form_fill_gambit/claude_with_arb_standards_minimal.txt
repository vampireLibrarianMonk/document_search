I propose the following for the description of proposed modification on the HOA Exterior Modification Application form:

I am contracting with American Home Contractors to replace the flat/low-slope roof on my home. The existing roof will be removed down to the roof decking. New 1" insulation board will be installed and anchored with screws and plates. A 2-ply self-adhering roofing system from GAF called LIBERTY SBS will be installed, consisting of a self-adhering base/ply sheet and a self-adhering granulated cap sheet in the color of my choice from GAF's standard LIBERTY color selections. All seams will be overlapped and all corners, edges and penetrations will be reinforced and flashed for a watertight installation. No torches, open flames, hot asphalt or solvent-based adhesives will be used, eliminating fumes and fire hazards. The new LIBERTY SBS roof system is backed by a manufacturer's limited warranty.
